# Copywriting

Clarity, usefulness, honest claims. For landing pages and articles. Do not invent numbers, reviews, or guarantees.

## Voice

Calm teacher: full sentences, cause → effect → what to do.

- The heading matches the section’s question. The body proves the heading.
- FAQ — like a support answer, 2–4 sentences.
- RU: «почему» for reasons, «зачем» only for purpose. No “Below: item, item”.
- EN: no `Below we cover`. Start with the subject.

## Before writing

If there is no brief: page goal, one primary CTA (for an article — what the reader can do at the end), who the reader is, the pain, how the product differs, what proof exists.

For an article from a finished `.md`, the brief is the source plus the fact-check table. Do not stop for a landing-page brief lock if the user already gave the article.

## Principles

- Clarity over cleverness
- Outcome → benefit → result, not a feature list
- Customer words, not corporate
- One idea per section
- No guarantees or “10x” unless it is in [project-facts.md](project-facts.md)

## Forbidden

Hype: seamless, cutting-edge, game-changer. Hollow intensifiers: very, extremely, truly — with no fact.
