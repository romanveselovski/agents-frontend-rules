# Narrative rewrite

After the SEO skeleton and before humanize. The article should read like a note to a colleague, not a keyword outline.

## Finished thought

| Avoid | Prefer |
|--------|--------|
| Three short phrases in any order | Context → problem → action → why |
| Bullets where prose teaches | Prose for “why”; lists for checklists, comparisons, step parameters |
| Heading + one line + list | 2–4 connected paragraphs; H3 inside a long step |
| FAQ under 15 words | 2–4 full sentences |
| “Mistakes” as nouns | What people do, what breaks, how to fix |

## Languages

Facts match. Word order is native, not a calque. RU — [ru-locale-style.md](ru-locale-style.md). UI labels as in the product.

## Discipline

No invented stats or reviews. Industry numbers — qualified and sourced. One idea per H2. No hype from [copywriting.md](copywriting.md).

## Self-check

- Every H2 except a checklist has a paragraph of 3+ sentences
- No section is only a list with no setup or wrap-up
- FAQs sound spoken
- Intro: who it is for and what they have at the end
- A second language does not copy the first language’s sentence order
