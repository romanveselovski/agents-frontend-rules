# Humanize

After [narrative-rewrite.md](narrative-rewrite.md). Full pass of [avoid-ai-writing](../../rules/avoid-ai-writing.mdc) (or `skills/avoid-ai-writing/SKILL.md`).

Mode: `edit` on the article file. Context: `blog`. Voice: `warm` or `professional`.

## What to check

All user-facing copy: headings, paragraphs, FAQ, steps, title, description, alt.

Do not rewrite: facts, prices, limits, code, UI labels, brands, label cells in a table.

If the text is still choppy — back to narrative-rewrite.

## Two passes

1. Teacher voice, em dashes `—` in prose → comma or two sentences, Tier 1 words, hollow intensifiers, “it’s not X, it’s Y” at most once.
2. Re-read the edits. Report “converged in 2 passes” or list exceptions.

For `ru`, then [ru-locale-style.md](ru-locale-style.md).
