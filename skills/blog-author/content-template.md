# Article skeleton

Storage format is anything (MDX, CMS, JSON). Fields below are meaning, not one repo’s contract.

## Per publish language

- `title` — H1, human-readable
- `metaTitle` — unique, about 60 characters
- `description` — 120–170 characters, why to read
- `coverImageAlt` — what is on the cover
- `seoKeywords` — up to 5 queries in the publish language
- sections, FAQ (from 3 if the profile uses FAQ schema), HowTo steps (if a guide)

## Block types

- Short answer (for a snippet) — 1–3 sentences
- H2/H3 heading
- Paragraph, list, callout, comparison table, image with width/height and alt

## Guide arc

1. What it is
2. Why the reader cares
3. How people usually do it and the downside (comparison optional)
4. How to do it here (H3 inside long steps)
5. Checklist: one intro sentence + numbered list
6. Mistakes — as paragraphs
7. Close and 2–3 internal links with meaningful anchors

Opinion/comparison without steps — HowTo is not required.

## Tags

Use the site’s tag dictionary. Do not invent one-off chips.
